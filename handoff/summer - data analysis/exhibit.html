Why this isn't a sensor artifact. The device recorded all 48 readings a day on every one of the 25 days from 28 November to 22 December — no outage, no restart, so nothing here is a power-on transient. And every channel moves together: the odour rise is accompanied by temperature flattening, CO₂ falling to the outdoor baseline and the noise floor dropping, then by cooling, CO₂ and noise all turning at once when she comes back. A sensor fault moves one channel; a room moves all of them.

Caveats. Mould odour is an uncalibrated machine-learning index in odour units, not a concentration; every figure here is relative to this room's own baseline. This is one home over one absence — illustrative, not a population estimate.
